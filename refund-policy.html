Refund & Cancellation Policy

How KivTech handles cancellations, payment verification and unsuccessful bundle processing.

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Before placing an order

Please confirm the package, price, validity and recipient Safaricom number before making payment. After selecting an offer, pay the exact displayed amount to KivTech automated M-PESA Buy Goods Till Number 5797885. Our 24/7 automated system is designed to dispense the selected offer immediately after successful payment. You should receive confirmation messages from KivTech and Safaricom. Contact us promptly if the offer or confirmations appear delayed, and do not make a second payment while the first is being checked.

Cancellation requests

If you wish to cancel an order, contact KivTech as soon as possible on WhatsApp 0715 024 923. A cancellation may not be possible once a bundle has been successfully processed or delivered. We will review each request based on its status and applicable law.

If payment is successful but processing fails

If payment is successful but the automated system does not deliver the requested bundle, KivTech will investigate and make a reasonable attempt to resolve the issue or retry the original package. If the retry is unsuccessful, we will contact you and may offer an available alternative bundle of comparable value or another mutually agreed adjustment. We will only process an alternative after you agree to it.

Incorrect details or duplicate payments

If you entered an incorrect recipient number, submitted duplicate payment details, or believe you paid more than once, contact us with the M-PESA transaction code and order information. Never share your M-PESA PIN or one-time password. We will investigate and explain the available resolution.
