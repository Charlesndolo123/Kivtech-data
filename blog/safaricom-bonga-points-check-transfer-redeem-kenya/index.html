Safaricom Bonga Points in Kenya: How to Check, Transfer and Redeem Them

By Charles Ndolo | KivTech Data Solutions · Updated 28 November 2026 · 8 min read

Safaricom Bonga Points are loyalty rewards earned by eligible subscribers. Customers often want to know how to check their points, share them with another number, redeem them for airtime or data, or use them to pay at participating businesses.

This guide separates Safaricom's official redemption options from claims made by independent cash-conversion websites. Bonga programme rules and reward options can change; always read the confirmation screen and current Safaricom terms before approving a transaction.

1. What are Bonga Points?

Bonga Points are part of Safaricom's loyalty programme for eligible PrePay and PostPay subscribers. Safaricom's published programme information explains how points are earned and redeemed for rewards. The points are loyalty units, not cash held in an M-PESA wallet.

2. How to check your Bonga Points balance

  1. On your Safaricom line, dial *126#.
  2. Choose the option for checking your Bonga balance or follow the current menu prompts.
  3. Review the balance and any expiry information returned.
  4. You can also open the Bonga section in the official MySafaricom app, where available.

Menu labels may change. Do not share your Bonga PIN or M-PESA PIN with anyone who claims they need it to check your balance.

3. How to redeem Bonga Points

Safaricom's published Bonga redemption terms describe redemption through USSD and the MySafaricom app. The available rewards can include airtime, data bundles, SMS, minutes, merchandise at selected outlets and payments through eligible Lipa na M-PESA Buy Goods or PayBill merchants.

  1. Dial *126# from your Safaricom line.
  2. Select the Bonga redemption option.
  3. Choose the reward category available to your line.
  4. Review the points required, reward amount and validity.
  5. Confirm only if the details are correct.

In the app, open the Bonga section and follow the current redemption prompts. The options shown may depend on your line type and the offers available at that time.

4. Can Bonga Points be converted directly to M-PESA cash?

Important: Safaricom's published Bonga programme information does not provide a standard direct redemption that deposits Bonga Points as cash into a customer's personal M-PESA wallet. Do not confuse paying a participating merchant through Lipa na Bonga with withdrawing cash.

Safaricom has publicly stated that direct cash conversion is not allowed. The official alternatives include redeeming points for airtime, bundles, minutes, merchandise and eligible merchant payments. Independent websites may advertise Bonga-to-cash services, but those are third-party offers, not an official Safaricom cash-out facility. Check their identity, payout rate, fees, privacy terms and complaints history before considering any transfer. Never disclose your M-PESA PIN, Bonga PIN or one-time password.

5. What is Lipa na Bonga?

Lipa na Bonga allows eligible customers to use points to pay participating merchants through supported Buy Goods till numbers or PayBill numbers. It is a way to spend points on eligible purchases, not a transfer of cash to your personal M-PESA wallet.

  1. Dial *126# and look for the Lipa na Bonga option.
  2. Enter the participating merchant's till or PayBill details as requested.
  3. Enter the payment amount and review the points required.
  4. Confirm the merchant details and transaction before entering your Bonga service PIN.
  5. Keep the confirmation message.

Safaricom's Lipa na Bonga terms state a redemption rate of KSh 0.20 per point, subject to change. Check the current rate displayed in the service before confirming.

6. Can you transfer Bonga Points to another Safaricom number?

Some Safaricom self-service menus and published customer guides describe a Bonga transfer option. Because transfer eligibility, minimum balances and limits can change, use the current *126# menu as the source of truth. If a transfer option is available, check the recipient number carefully, review the points amount and any conditions shown, and confirm only when you are certain. If the option is absent, contact Safaricom support rather than relying on an old code or an unofficial agent.

Safety reminder: A legitimate points transfer should not require you to tell another person your PIN. Enter confidential credentials only into Safaricom's official USSD or app flow.

7. Do Bonga Points expire?

Safaricom's published terms state that points older than three years expire, and points may also be lost if a line is deleted after inactivity under the applicable service terms. Check the expiry details shown in your account and redeem eligible points before they expire.

Frequently asked questions

Which code checks Bonga Points?

Dial *126# and follow the current Bonga balance menu prompts.

Can I use Bonga Points to buy data?

Safaricom's published redemption terms include data bundles among the possible rewards, subject to eligibility and the options currently offered.

Can I send Bonga Points to a friend?

Check whether a transfer option is available in the current *126# menu and follow its displayed eligibility rules. Confirm the recipient number before authorising.

Is Lipa na Bonga the same as cash conversion?

No. It lets you pay participating merchants using points. It does not mean the points are deposited into your personal M-PESA wallet as cash.

Bottom line: Use Safaricom's official menu or app to check and redeem points. Be cautious of anyone promising guaranteed Bonga cash conversion or asking for your PIN.

Official reference: Safaricom Bonga redemption terms and Lipa na Bonga service terms.
