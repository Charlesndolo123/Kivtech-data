How to Sambaza Safaricom Data to Another Number in Kenya

By Charles Ndolo | KivTech Data Solutions · Updated 28 November 2026 · 6 min read

If a friend or family member runs out of internet, you may be able to share some of your eligible Safaricom data with them instead of buying a separate bundle. Safaricom calls this service Data Sambaza. It moves part of an eligible data balance from one Safaricom line to another, subject to the service conditions.

Quick answer: Safaricom's published Data Sambaza instructions say to dial *544#, choose Data Sambaza, enter the amount in MB and then enter the recipient's Safaricom number. Check the live menu and all details before confirming.

What is Safaricom Data Sambaza?

Data Sambaza is a Safaricom service for transferring eligible data bundles between Safaricom mobile lines. The person sending the data is the donor, while the line receiving it is the recipient. It is different from buying a fresh bundle for another person: Sambaza moves data you already have, while a purchase for another number charges you for a new bundle.

Who can use Data Sambaza?

Safaricom's published Data Sambaza terms state that the service is available to PrePay and PostPay subscribers, subject to exclusions. The document says that a line cannot use the service while roaming or while inactive, expired, suspended or deleted. It also says corporate lines may receive shared data but cannot send it to other lines.

Eligibility and service conditions can be revised. If the option is missing from your menu, check with Safaricom rather than assuming your line is faulty.

How to Sambaza data using *544#

  1. On the Safaricom line that has the data you want to share, open your phone dialler.
  2. Dial *544# and send the request.
  3. Choose the menu option labelled Data Sambaza.
  4. Enter the amount of data you want to transfer, in MB, if the menu asks for it.
  5. Enter the recipient's Safaricom mobile number carefully.
  6. Read the confirmation details. Check the recipient number and amount before approving the transfer.
  7. Wait for the confirmation message. The sender and recipient should check their messages and data balances.

Safaricom's published instructions describe this USSD route. Menu wording or availability may change, so use the options currently displayed on your phone. Do not proceed if the details shown do not match your intention.

Can you Sambaza data by SMS?

Safaricom's published Data Sambaza document also describes an SMS method. It gives the format as the amount in MB, followed by an asterisk and the recipient's mobile number, sent to 450. For example, the document illustrates the format with 10*0722000000.

Important: The published PDF does not show a clear recent revision date and includes old illustrative examples. Treat the SMS format as a method documented by Safaricom, not a guarantee that every line can still use it today. The safest starting point is the live *544# menu. If the SMS route fails or is not confirmed by Safaricom, do not keep retrying blindly.

Which data bundles can be transferred?

Safaricom's Data Sambaza terms distinguish eligible volume data bundles from other data products. The document says promotional or bonus data cannot be transferred and excludes daily data bundles from the Sambaza service. Check the current bundle conditions and the options shown on your line because not every data balance is necessarily shareable.

How much data can you send?

The Data Sambaza PDF currently published on Safaricom's website lists a minimum transfer of 5 MB, a maximum of 10 MB per session and a daily limit of 20 MB. It also lists a maximum of two requests per day and twenty transfers per month.

These figures appear in a document that does not display a clear current revision date. Safaricom can change service conditions, so confirm the limits in the live USSD menu or with customer care before relying on them. If the menu displays a different limit, follow the current instruction shown by Safaricom.

Does transferred data expire?

Safaricom's published terms say transferred data carries the donor's validity. They also describe how the recipient's existing bundle validity may affect the date shown on the receiving line. In practical terms, do not assume that sharing data gives the recipient a fresh validity period. Check the confirmation message and the recipient's balance after the transfer.

Can you reverse a Data Sambaza transfer?

Safaricom's published Data Sambaza terms say a completed transfer cannot be reversed. That makes checking the recipient number especially important. If you send data to the wrong number, contact Safaricom promptly for guidance, but do not assume the transfer can be recovered.

Data Sambaza vs buying a bundle for another number

These options solve slightly different problems:

  • Data Sambaza: transfers some eligible data already available on your line to another Safaricom number.
  • Buy for Other Number: lets you purchase a new data bundle for a different Safaricom number, using the payment method offered in the menu.

If you have no shareable data balance, buying a bundle for the other number may be the more suitable route. Safaricom's published PrePay and PostPay bundle terms describe a separate purchase path through *544#, where you select Buy Data Bundles, choose Buy for Other Number, enter the recipient number, select a bundle and follow the payment prompts. Check the current menu before paying.

What to do if Data Sambaza is not working

  • Check the donor line: make sure it is active and not roaming.
  • Check the bundle type: promotional, bonus and some special bundles may not qualify.
  • Check the amount: the menu may enforce a minimum or maximum transfer.
  • Check the recipient: confirm that the number is a Safaricom line and that you typed it correctly.
  • Wait for confirmation: do not assume a transfer succeeded just because you submitted a request.
  • Contact Safaricom: if data appears to have been deducted but the recipient has not received it, use Safaricom's official customer-care channels and provide the transaction details.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Safaricom Data Sambaza code?

Safaricom's published instructions direct subscribers to dial *544# and select Data Sambaza. Check the live menu in case the option or wording has changed.

Can I Sambaza data to Airtel or Telkom?

The published service is described as a transfer between Safaricom lines. It is not documented as a way to send your Safaricom data balance to an Airtel or Telkom number.

Can I share free or promotional data?

Safaricom's published Data Sambaza terms say bonus and promotional data are not transferable through this service. Check the terms attached to any special offer you receive.

Can I transfer data while roaming?

The published Data Sambaza terms list roaming subscribers among those who cannot use the service.

Is Data Sambaza the same as buying data for someone else?

No. Sambaza transfers eligible data you already have. Buying a bundle for another number is a separate purchase and uses the payment option shown during checkout.

Final thoughts

Data Sambaza can be useful when someone close to you needs a small amount of internet and you have an eligible Safaricom bundle to share. Start with *544#, follow the Data Sambaza prompts and double-check the amount and recipient before confirming. Because Safaricom's online Sambaza PDF contains older examples and does not show a clear revision date, use the live menu or Safaricom customer care to verify limits and conditions before making a transfer.
