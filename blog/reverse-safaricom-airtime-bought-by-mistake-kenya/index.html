Bought Safaricom Airtime by Mistake? How to Request a Reversal in Kenya

By Charles Ndolo | KivTech Data Solutions · Updated 28 November 2026 · 6 min read

Imagine you meant to send KSh 1,000 to someone through M-PESA but accidentally selected Buy Airtime. The airtime is now on your Safaricom line, while the money has left your M-PESA wallet. If this happens, act quickly and avoid using the airtime while you check the official reversal options.

1. First confirm how the airtime was purchased

This guide focuses on airtime bought using M-PESA through the Buy Airtime option. Keep the original M-PESA confirmation SMS. It contains the transaction reference Safaricom may need to trace the purchase.

2. What to do immediately

  1. Do not make calls, buy bundles or use the airtime if you can avoid it.
  2. Check the airtime balance and compare it with the amount purchased.
  3. Keep the original M-PESA transaction message intact.
  4. Use Safaricom's official self-service reversal instruction or contact Safaricom support for help.

3. How to request reversal through 456

Safaricom has advised customers who accidentally purchase airtime through M-PESA to forward the original M-PESA confirmation message to 456 for reversal assistance. Follow the response messages and instructions from Safaricom. Do not forward a message to a number supplied by an unknown caller or social-media account.

Example: If you mistakenly buy KSh 1,000 airtime, keep the airtime unused and forward the original M-PESA purchase confirmation to 456, following Safaricom's current instructions.

4. What if some of the airtime has already been used?

Safaricom has previously explained that where airtime has been consumed, including through calls, subscriptions or Okoa debt, a customer may need to top up the used amount so the airtime balance is restored to the original purchase amount before forwarding the original transaction message to 456. A recent Safaricom response reported by Kenyan media also says the remaining airtime balance should be equal to or greater than the amount purchased.

Reversal is subject to Safaricom's checks and approval. Do not assume every request will be successful or that the same conditions apply to every transaction type. Contact Safaricom through its official channels if the automated process does not resolve the issue.

5. What information should you have ready?

  • The original M-PESA confirmation SMS.
  • The transaction code and date/time.
  • The amount of airtime purchased.
  • Your current airtime balance.
  • The Safaricom number used for the purchase.

6. Avoid common reversal scams

  • Never disclose your M-PESA PIN, Bonga PIN or one-time password.
  • Do not install remote-access apps at the request of someone claiming to be Safaricom support.
  • Do not send money to a person promising to reverse the airtime manually.
  • Use only Safaricom's official 456 process and verified customer-care channels.
  • Keep screenshots and SMS records until the case is resolved.

Frequently asked questions

Can I reverse KSh 1,000 airtime bought by mistake?

You can submit a reversal request if the purchase was made through M-PESA, but approval depends on Safaricom's checks and the airtime balance conditions. Start with the original transaction SMS and the 456 instructions.

What if I have already used part of the airtime?

Safaricom has previously advised customers to restore the used amount so the airtime balance reaches the original purchase amount before sending the original message to 456. Confirm the current requirement with Safaricom.

Does this apply to airtime sent to the wrong phone number?

This article covers airtime purchased for your own line through M-PESA. Airtime sent to another number may follow a different process. Contact Safaricom with the transaction details.

Is reversal guaranteed?

No. Submitting a request does not guarantee a reversal. Safaricom must verify the transaction and apply its current rules.

Bottom line: If you accidentally buy airtime instead of sending M-PESA, stop using the airtime, keep the original transaction SMS and follow Safaricom's official 456 reversal process promptly.
