How to Buy Safaricom Data Bundles Using Bonga Points in Kenya

By Charles Ndolo | KivTech Data Solutions · Updated 28 November 2026 · 5 min read

You do not always need to pay for mobile data with airtime or M-PESA. Safaricom's Bonga programme includes data bundles among the rewards that eligible members can redeem with points. If you have accumulated Bonga Points, you can check the available data reward on your line and decide whether it suits your needs.

Quick answer: Start by dialling *126#, open the Bonga redemption menu and check whether a data-bundle option is available. You can also check the Bonga section in the MySafaricom app. Read the data amount, points required and validity shown before confirming.

Before you redeem Bonga Points for data

Make sure you are using the Safaricom line that holds the Bonga Points. Check your points balance first and confirm that the points are available for redemption. Safaricom's published terms say Bonga rewards include mobile data, but the offers shown to you may depend on the programme rules and your line's eligibility.

It is also worth checking whether you need data immediately or whether an ordinary bundle paid for with airtime or M-PESA would better match your usage. A points reward is useful only if its size and validity fit what you plan to do online.

Method 1: Use the *126# USSD menu

  1. Open the phone dialler on the Safaricom line with your Bonga balance.
  2. Dial *126# and send the request.
  3. Choose the Bonga redemption option. Safaricom's published instructions identify this as Redeem Bonga Points.
  4. Look through the reward categories and select the data or data-bundle option if it appears.
  5. Review the available data offer. Check how many points it costs and how long the data will remain valid.
  6. Only proceed if the details are correct and you want that specific offer.
  7. Wait for Safaricom's confirmation message and keep it until you have checked that the bundle has been credited.

The wording and order of USSD options can change. Follow the menu displayed during your session rather than relying on a numbered option copied from an old guide.

Method 2: Use the MySafaricom app

  1. Open the official MySafaricom app and sign in to the relevant line.
  2. Find and open the Bonga section.
  3. Choose the redemption or rewards area.
  4. Check whether data bundles are listed among the available rewards.
  5. Open the data offer you want and review the points required, data amount and validity.
  6. Confirm only when the displayed details match what you intended to redeem.

Safaricom's published Bonga instructions describe the app route as selecting Bonga and redeeming resources according to preference. The exact labels in the app may change as it is updated.

How many Bonga Points do you need?

There is no single points amount that should be assumed for every data offer. The number of points required depends on the bundle options available at the time you redeem. Safaricom has published Bonga Bundles terms with specific data-and-points combinations, but older offer tables should not be treated as a guarantee that the same packages are still available today.

For an accurate answer, use the live *126# menu or MySafaricom app and compare the points cost shown there with your balance. Do not confirm a redemption based on a price or points table from an undated social media post.

How long does Bonga data last?

Check the validity period displayed beside the data reward before you accept it. Safaricom's published Bonga Bundles terms describe a seven-day validity period for the offers covered by those terms. However, the offer currently shown on your line is the one to rely on; do not assume that every future Bonga data promotion has identical conditions.

Important: Redeemed Bonga data is not the same as a cash balance. Check the bundle's expiry and usage conditions, and do not assume unused data can be transferred, refunded or carried forward unless Safaricom's current terms explicitly say so.

What if the data option is missing?

If you cannot see a data reward, do not keep selecting unrelated options or use a shortcut from an unofficial website. Check that you are on the correct Safaricom line, reopen the Bonga section, and consult Safaricom customer care if the option remains unavailable. Safaricom's Bonga and data terms may distinguish between subscriber types and service offers, so ask the provider to confirm whether your line qualifies.

You can also check whether Safaricom presents a Bonga data redemption prompt through its Data Manager when you are approaching or have exhausted your normal bundle. Safaricom's published data-bundle terms describe such a prompt as one possible way to redeem Bonga Points for data. Availability depends on the service and the options offered to your line.

How to check that your Bonga data was credited

  • Wait for the confirmation SMS after the redemption.
  • Use Safaricom's data-balance option or the MySafaricom app to check your bundle balance.
  • Read the confirmation carefully for the data amount and expiry date.
  • If points were deducted but the data does not appear, contact Safaricom through its official customer-care channels and provide the transaction details.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Choosing too quickly: Check the points cost, data size and validity before approving.
  • Using the wrong SIM: Make sure the active Safaricom line is the one with the Bonga balance you intend to use.
  • Trusting old bundle tables: Offers and programme conditions can be revised.
  • Sharing account secrets: Never disclose your Bonga PIN, M-PESA PIN or one-time password to someone claiming to redeem points for you.
  • Ignoring expiry: Use the data before the validity period ends.

Frequently asked questions

Which code can I use to redeem Bonga Points for data?

Safaricom's published Bonga redemption instructions say to dial *126#, select Redeem Bonga Points and then choose the preferred reward category. Select data if it is offered on your line.

Can I use the MySafaricom app instead?

Yes. Safaricom's published instructions describe opening Bonga in the app and redeeming resources according to your preference.

Can every Safaricom subscriber redeem Bonga Points for data?

Do not assume that every line has identical offers. Safaricom's programme terms set eligibility conditions for particular Bonga services. Check the option on your own line or ask Safaricom to confirm your eligibility.

Can I transfer the Bonga data to another number?

Do not assume that redeemed Bonga data can be transferred. Check the conditions attached to the specific bundle. The Bonga Bundles terms we reviewed state that the data bundles covered by those terms cannot be transferred through Sambaza.

Do Bonga Points expire?

Safaricom's published Bonga redemption terms state that points older than three years expire. Check your account and the latest programme terms for the rules that apply to your points.

Final thoughts

Using Bonga Points for data can be a practical way to get online without paying for that particular bundle with airtime or M-PESA. Start with *126# or the Bonga section in MySafaricom, compare the live offer with your needs, and confirm only after checking the points cost and expiry. When an option is missing or unclear, Safaricom is the right place to verify the current rule.
