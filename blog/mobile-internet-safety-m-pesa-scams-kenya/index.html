Mobile Internet Safety in Kenya: How to Avoid Common M-PESA and Online Scams

KivTech Data Solutions · 2026-11-25 · 6 min read

Mobile services make it easier to shop, communicate and pay bills, but scammers also use calls, SMS, social media and fake websites to target customers. A few simple habits can reduce the risk of losing money or personal information.

Never share your M-PESA PIN

Your PIN is private. A genuine support agent should not ask you to disclose it. Do not share one-time passwords, account recovery codes or screen-sharing access with strangers.

Check payment details before confirming

When making a payment, verify the recipient name, Till or PayBill details and amount on the M-PESA confirmation screen. Do not rely only on a number sent in a forwarded message.

Be careful with urgent messages

Messages claiming your account will be blocked, a prize will expire or a payment must be reversed immediately are common pressure tactics. Pause and verify through the provider’s official channels.

Inspect links before opening them

Look carefully at website spelling and domain names. Avoid logging in through links sent by unknown numbers. Open the service’s official website or app yourself instead.

Do not install unknown apps

Avoid APK files and apps sent through chat by people you do not know. Install apps from trusted stores and review requested permissions.

Keep your phone protected

Use a screen lock, update your operating system and apps, and enable account recovery options. If your phone is lost, contact your mobile operator and financial provider promptly.

What to do if you suspect fraud

Stop communicating with the suspected scammer. Contact the relevant service provider through its official support channel, preserve messages and transaction details, and follow the provider’s reporting instructions.

Helpful reminder: Offer availability, prices, validity and device specifications can change. Always confirm current details with the relevant provider or seller before making a purchase.

Frequently asked questions

Will Safaricom or a bank ask for my PIN?

Do not disclose your PIN to anyone claiming to be support. Use official channels and follow the provider’s published security guidance.

Is every cheap online offer a scam?

No, but unusually urgent or unrealistic offers deserve extra scrutiny. Verify the seller, payment details and terms before paying.

Should I send an M-PESA confirmation screenshot to support?

Only share transaction information with the verified business support channel when needed for order verification. Hide unrelated personal information.
