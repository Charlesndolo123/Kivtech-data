How to Check Your Safaricom Data Balance in Kenya

By Charles Ndolo | KivTech Data Solutions

Running out of mobile data unexpectedly can interrupt your work, online learning, entertainment and communication. Fortunately, Safaricom provides ways for customers to monitor their remaining data bundles and plan their internet usage.

In this guide, we explain how to check your Safaricom data balance in Kenya, understand bundle validity and manage your internet consumption more effectively.

1. Check Your Data Balance Using USSD

One convenient way to check your Safaricom data balance is through the USSD self-service menu.

  1. Open the phone dialler on your device.
  2. Dial *544# using your Safaricom line.
  3. Follow the instructions displayed on your screen.
  4. Look for the data balance or bundle balance option.
  5. Check your remaining data and expiry information where provided.

USSD menu options can change. Always follow the latest instructions displayed by Safaricom.

2. Use the MySafaricom App

Another convenient option is the official MySafaricom app. It allows customers to access account information and manage various Safaricom services.

  1. Open the MySafaricom app on your smartphone.
  2. Sign in using your Safaricom number if required.
  3. Navigate to the account, home or usage section.
  4. Look for your active data bundles and remaining balance.

The app interface may change following updates, but it provides a useful way to monitor your Safaricom account.

3. Always Check Your Bundle Expiry Date

Knowing how much data remains is important, but checking its validity is equally important.

Different Safaricom bundles have different validity periods. Some expire within hours, while others remain active for several days or longer.

KivTech Tip: Always check both your remaining data and its expiry time before purchasing another bundle.

4. What Makes Your Data Finish Quickly?

Some activities consume mobile data faster than others:

  • Watching videos on YouTube and other streaming platforms.
  • Making video calls.
  • Downloading large files.
  • Automatic application updates.
  • Social media applications that automatically play videos.
  • Background data usage by installed applications.

To save data, reduce video quality, disable unnecessary automatic downloads and monitor which applications consume the most data.

5. What Should You Do When Your Data Runs Out?

When your bundle is depleted, you can purchase another suitable package depending on your internet needs and budget.

Before purchasing, compare the available data allocation, price and validity period. This helps you select a package that suits your browsing habits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which code can I dial to check my Safaricom data balance?

Dial *544# and follow the current USSD menu instructions to locate your data balance.

Can I check my Safaricom data balance without internet?

Yes. You can use the USSD service by dialling *544# without needing an active internet connection.

Why does my Safaricom data finish quickly?

Video streaming, downloads, automatic updates and background application activity can consume significant amounts of data. Check your phone's data usage settings to identify applications using the most data.

Can I check my bundle expiry date?

Yes. Follow the available balance and bundle information options through Safaricom's self-service menu or official app.
