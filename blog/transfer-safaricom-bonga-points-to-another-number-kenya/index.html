How to Transfer Safaricom Bonga Points to Another Number in Kenya

By Charles Ndolo | KivTech Data Solutions · Updated 28 November 2026 · 6 min read

Want to share your Safaricom Bonga Points with a friend or family member? Bonga transfer instructions have appeared in Safaricom programme terms, but the transfer details are not included in the current Bonga redemption terms we could verify on Safaricom's website. This guide explains the USSD route documented in an older copy and how to check whether the option is still available on your line.

Important update: Safaricom's currently published Bonga redemption terms explain how to redeem points but do not confirm the current transfer menu, transfer limits or fees. Treat the detailed transfer rules below as historical guidance, not a guarantee of today's service conditions. The live *126# menu and Safaricom customer care are the best places to confirm availability.

1. Can you transfer Bonga Points to another number?

Safaricom's older Bonga Loyalty Programme terms included a section allowing enrolled subscribers to transfer points to another enrolled subscriber. However, the current official Bonga redemption document available on Safaricom's website does not include that transfer section. This means we cannot independently confirm from the current published terms whether the same transfer option and conditions remain active today.

2. How to check for the Bonga transfer option

  1. Use the Safaricom SIM that holds the Bonga Points.
  2. Dial *126#.
  3. Read the menu carefully and look for an option labelled Transfer Bonga Points.
  4. If the option appears, select it and follow the on-screen instructions.
  5. If it does not appear, do not rely on an old shortcut or a third-party agent. Contact Safaricom through its official customer-care channels to confirm whether transfers are currently supported for your line.

3. Transfer steps documented in older Bonga terms

An older copy of Safaricom's Bonga programme terms describes the following USSD process. We include it as a reference only; the exact menu numbering and availability may have changed.

  1. Dial *126# and select the transfer option if it is shown. The older document labels it option 4 and also lists *126*4# as a shortcut.
  2. Enter the recipient's Safaricom mobile number.
  3. Enter the number of Bonga Points you intend to send.
  4. Enter your Bonga PIN when prompted by the official USSD session.
  5. Review the details and confirm only if the recipient number and points amount are correct.
  6. Wait for the Safaricom confirmation message and check your balance.
Before you confirm: Verify every digit of the recipient's number. The older terms say completed transfers cannot be reversed. If the live menu displays different steps or conditions, follow the live Safaricom instructions instead.

4. What were the older transfer limits?

The older copy of the programme terms listed these conditions:

  • A sender needed at least 100 Bonga Points before making a transfer.
  • The minimum transfer per attempt was 50 points.
  • The sender had to retain at least 50 points after the transfer.
  • Both sender and recipient had to be enrolled in the Bonga programme.
  • Corporate-account lines and fraud-locked accounts were listed as ineligible.
  • The older document stated that Safaricom did not charge a transfer service fee and that transferred points could not be reversed.

These are historical conditions, not verified current limits. Do not plan a transfer around these numbers unless Safaricom confirms that they still apply to your account.

5. What if you do not have a Bonga PIN?

The older programme document describes a Bonga PIN setup and reset process, but the current published redemption terms we checked do not provide current PIN-reset instructions for point transfers. If the live transfer menu asks for a Bonga PIN you do not have, stop and use Safaricom's official support or visit a Safaricom Shop for guidance. Never give your PIN to another person, including someone offering to transfer points for you.

6. How to stay safe when sharing Bonga Points

  • Start the process yourself from your own Safaricom SIM.
  • Use only the official *126# USSD menu or the official MySafaricom app.
  • Never share your Bonga PIN, M-PESA PIN or one-time password.
  • Do not send money to a stranger who promises to transfer points for you.
  • Check the recipient number, points amount and any fee shown before confirming.
  • Keep the confirmation SMS in case you need to contact Safaricom.

7. Frequently asked questions

What is the code for transferring Bonga Points?

An older copy of Safaricom's programme terms lists *126# and the transfer menu, with *126*4# as a shortcut. Because the current published terms we found do not confirm that shortcut, first check the live *126# menu.

Can I transfer Bonga Points to Airtel or Telkom?

The older programme terms describe transfers between enrolled Bonga subscribers. They do not establish a transfer to Airtel or Telkom numbers. Confirm recipient eligibility in the current Safaricom menu.

Can transferred Bonga Points be reversed?

The older terms say completed transfers cannot be reversed. Treat every confirmation as final unless Safaricom's current terms state otherwise.

Is there a transfer fee?

The older terms stated there was no service fee, but we could not verify a current fee rule in Safaricom's latest published redemption terms. Check the live confirmation screen before proceeding.

What if I cannot see the transfer option?

Do not assume the old shortcut still works. Contact Safaricom using the official contact details on its website and ask whether Bonga-to-Bonga transfers are currently enabled for your line.

Bottom line: Check *126# first. The transfer process and limits often repeated online come from older programme terms, so verify the option and conditions with Safaricom before sending points. Protect your PIN and confirm the recipient carefully.

Official reference: Safaricom Bonga Loyalty Programme and redemption terms (PDF). Historical transfer instructions: older copy of Bonga programme terms hosted on Scribd; this is not Safaricom's current official source.
