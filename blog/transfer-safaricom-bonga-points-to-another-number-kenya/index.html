How to Share Safaricom Bonga Points with Another Number in Kenya

By Charles Ndolo | KivTech Data Solutions · Updated 28 November 2026 · 5 min read

Bonga Points are usually associated with the Safaricom line on which they were earned. If you want to help a relative or friend, you may be looking for a way to move some of those points to their number. The important first step is to check whether Safaricom currently offers a transfer option on your line. This guide shows you how to check safely and what to do if the option is missing.

Accuracy note: Safaricom's official Bonga redemption document explains how to redeem points through *126# and the MySafaricom app. The document available on Safaricom's website does not set out a Bonga-to-Bonga transfer procedure, transfer shortcut, limits or fee. We therefore will not present old instructions circulating online as confirmed current rules.

Start by checking the official Bonga menu

  1. Use the Safaricom SIM that holds the points.
  2. Dial *126#.
  3. Read the options displayed on your phone. Look for a menu item that specifically refers to transferring or sharing Bonga Points.
  4. If you see that option, open it and read every instruction shown by Safaricom before continuing.
  5. If no transfer option appears, stop there and ask Safaricom whether the service is currently available for your line.

USSD menus can be changed by the service provider. For that reason, the options displayed during your own session are more useful than an old screenshot, a menu number copied from a blog, or a shortcut you cannot verify.

If Safaricom displays a transfer option

Follow the instructions presented in the official session rather than relying on a fixed sequence published elsewhere. Before approving anything, check these details:

  • Recipient: Make sure the destination is the intended Safaricom number. Read the digits twice.
  • Amount: Confirm the number of points you intend to send and the balance that will remain.
  • Security: Enter a PIN only inside the genuine Safaricom USSD session or another official Safaricom channel.
  • Confirmation: Read the final prompt carefully. Keep the confirmation message if the transaction goes through.
Our practical advice: Do not confirm a transaction while distracted or while someone else is directing you over a call or chat. End the conversation and start again yourself using the official menu.

What transfer limits or charges should you expect?

We could not verify a current transfer minimum, maximum, daily cap, eligibility rule or service fee in the official Safaricom Bonga redemption document linked below. Numbers repeated on older websites may refer to a previous version of the programme. Check the terms displayed by Safaricom before making a decision, and do not assume that a previously published limit still applies.

The same caution applies to claims that transfers are always free or can never be reversed. Ask Safaricom to confirm the current conditions before you proceed.

What if the transfer option is not available?

If your *126# menu does not show a transfer feature, do not try random USSD codes or give your details to a person offering to move the points for you. Contact Safaricom through the customer-care options published on its official website, or visit a Safaricom Shop. Ask specifically whether Bonga Points can currently be transferred from your line to another Safaricom subscriber.

You can still check the rewards Safaricom documents for Bonga redemption. Its published terms describe redemption through *126# or the MySafaricom app for eligible rewards such as airtime, data, SMS and minutes. The available options depend on the service and your line.

Keep your Bonga Points and account secure

  • Never disclose your Bonga PIN, M-PESA PIN or one-time password to another person.
  • Do not share an OTP or approve a prompt you did not initiate.
  • Avoid links and phone numbers sent by strangers who claim they can transfer points for you.
  • Use Safaricom's own menu, app, website or shop for account-related help.
  • If you suspect someone has accessed your line or account, contact Safaricom promptly.

Frequently asked questions

Which code should I use to check Bonga services?

Safaricom's published redemption instructions say to dial *126#, choose “Redeem Bonga Points” and then select the reward category. The MySafaricom app also has a Bonga section.

Can I send Bonga Points to another Safaricom number?

We cannot confirm the current transfer availability from the official redemption document we reviewed. Check the live *126# menu or ask Safaricom directly before promising points to someone else.

What is the Bonga transfer shortcut?

We are not publishing an unverified shortcut. Use the main *126# menu and follow the options that Safaricom currently displays.

How many Bonga Points can I transfer?

A current transfer limit was not specified in the official redemption document we checked. Confirm any minimum, maximum or daily cap with Safaricom before proceeding.

Will Safaricom charge me to transfer points?

We could not verify a current transfer fee from the official document. Check the on-screen terms or ask Safaricom customer care.

Final word

If you want to share Bonga Points, begin with the official *126# menu and let Safaricom's current instructions guide you. Verify the recipient, amount and conditions before approving a transaction. Where the transfer option or its rules are unclear, get confirmation directly from Safaricom rather than relying on old online instructions.
