Privacy Policy

How this website handles information.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KivTech Data Solutions handles information when you visit this website, browse offers, or contact us. We aim to collect only information needed to respond to enquiries, verify payments and process requested bundles. This policy must be updated if our actual website tools or data practices change.

Information you provide

When you place an order or contact us, you may provide a recipient Safaricom number, M-PESA transaction code, and contact details or message content. We use these details to identify your order, verify payment, deliver the requested service, and respond to support requests. Do not send passwords, M-PESA PINs, one-time passwords or other confidential authentication details.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

This website may use essential browser storage or similar technologies needed for basic operation and user experience. If we enable analytics, advertising, or third-party measurement tools, those services may use cookies, web beacons, device identifiers or similar technologies to measure traffic, prevent fraud, personalise or serve advertisements, and understand site performance. Third-party advertising providers, including Google if Google advertising products are enabled, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s visits to this or other websites, subject to their own policies and the user’s settings. We will identify and configure the actual services used on the live site and provide any consent choices required by applicable law.

Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell personal information. We may share the minimum information needed with service providers or relevant payment and telecommunications partners where required to verify a transaction, process an order, secure the website or comply with law. Links to WhatsApp, payment services and other third-party websites are governed by those providers’ own privacy policies. We do not control their services.

Retention and security

We keep order and support information only for as long as reasonably needed for service delivery, customer support, record keeping and legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and questions

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have shared or request correction where appropriate. Some records may need to be retained to resolve an order or meet legal obligations.

Security and contact

We take reasonable steps to avoid requesting sensitive payment credentials through this website. Never share your M-PESA PIN with anyone. For privacy questions, contact KivTech Data Solutions on 0715 024 923 or support@kivtechdatasolutions.co.ke.

Updates

This policy may be updated when website features or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Important: Before applying for advertising services such as Google AdSense, review and tailor this policy to accurately describe the cookies, analytics, advertising partners and data practices actually enabled on the live website. This draft is not a substitute for that review.
