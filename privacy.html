How this website handles information.

This policy should be revised to identify the actual services used before they are activated.

We take reasonable steps to avoid requesting sensitive payment credentials through this website. Never share your M-PESA PIN with anyone. For privacy questions, contact Kivtech Data Solutions on 0715 024 923.

Updates
